Engage in the captivating Same Colored Ball Game, a delightful color matching adventure that entertains and challenges your cognitive abilities! Seamlessly match the colored balls and gather them until all identical colors unite within the same tube. It's a stimulating yet soothing game designed to exercise your brain while providing endless enjoyment!

HOW TO PLAY:

• Simply tap any colored ball traversing the dash.

• Remember, you can only select a ball if it matches the color of the previously chosen one; otherwise, it will be rejected.

• Need to start afresh? You can restart the level at any time or meticulously retrace your steps using the back button.

• Your objective? Stack all the balls with identical colors together.
