Play Bounce
Play Game

Explore the game of 'one bounce,' a football (soccer ball) game where players pass the ball with a maximum of one bounce allowed between passes.

Challenge yourself and other players to bounce balls into a grid simultaneously or in turns. Bounce the balls on the table before aiming for the grid. The first to achieve four balls of the same color in a row wins the game.

Experience buoyancy, the upward force exerted by a liquid on an immersed object. When you immerse an object in water, you'll feel an upward force acting on it, intensifying as you push the object deeper into the water.
