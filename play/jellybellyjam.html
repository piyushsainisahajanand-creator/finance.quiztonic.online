Play Game

Dive into the sugary world of Jelly Belly Jam, a delightful puzzle game packed with scrumptious desserts. Form rows or columns with 3 or more treats of the same color to watch them grow to their full size, triggering delightful chain reactions. Challenge yourself in this joyful saga solo or compete with friends to see who can achieve the highest score!

☆☆☆☆☆ How to play ☆☆☆☆☆

1. Create rows or columns with 3 or more desserts of the same color to make them expand.

2. Click or drag desserts to swap their positions.

3. Aim to expand the desserts to their full size and outsmart the greedy mouse.
